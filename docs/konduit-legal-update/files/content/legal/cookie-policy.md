# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

## Our approach

Konduit uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. We do not serve advertising and do not use tracking cookies.

## Essential cookies

We may use strictly necessary cookies for site functionality (such as form submission state and the cookie notice preference stored in local storage). These do not track you and cannot be disabled without breaking core features.

## Managing cookies

Because we do not use tracking cookies, there is nothing to opt out of beyond clearing your browser's local storage. You can configure your browser to block all cookies, though this may affect site functionality.
