# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Konduit is a trading name of Digiteq Holdings Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 03730207). Our registered office is 66 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NA, United Kingdom.

For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, Digiteq Holdings Limited is the data controller. We also align our practices with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## What we collect

When you use Konduit, we may collect:

- Your name and company name
- Email address
- Phone or WhatsApp number
- Country of delivery
- Product requirements and any details you provide
- Partner application details (services offered, team size, certifications)

We also collect standard analytics data (pages visited, referral source, device type) through Plausible Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not collect personal data.

**Technical data.** Our hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard server logs including IP addresses, request timestamps, and user agent strings. These are retained for operational and security purposes.

## What we do not collect

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising. We do not use Google Analytics. We do not run retargeting pixels. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

## How we use your data

We use your information to respond to enquiries, deliver our services, and improve the Konduit experience. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for automated decision-making.

Specifically, we use data for:

- responding to quote requests and contact enquiries
- processing partner applications
- understanding aggregate site usage patterns to improve our content
- maintaining the security and performance of our website

## Third-party processors

We use the following services to process data on our behalf:

- **Vercel** (hosting and deployment)
- **HubSpot** (CRM and form handling)
- **Resend** (email delivery)
- **Plausible Analytics** (website analytics)

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. You can also object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us at privacy@konduit.tech to exercise these rights.
