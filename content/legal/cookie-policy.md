# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

## Our approach

Digiteq uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. We do not serve advertising and do not use tracking cookies.

## Essential cookies

We may use strictly necessary cookies for site functionality (such as form submission state and authentication). These do not track you and cannot be disabled without breaking core features.
